At the USDA Agricultural Research Service's Livestock and Range Research Laboratory, I supported GIS-based risk assessment and land-use planning on rangeland systems.

The Livestock and Range Research Laboratory, also known as Fort Keogh, is a USDA Agricultural Research Service facility of about 55,000 acres in Miles City, Montana. It is operated with the Montana Agricultural Experiment Station and studies beef cattle production and rangeland ecology, including fire, drought, weeds, and soil. Research in semi-arid grasslands depends on clean, well-documented spatial data, which was the focus of my internship.

- Collected, cleaned, and validated spatial datasets for downstream analysis.
- Supported GIS-based risk assessment and land-use planning workflows.
- Contributed field and geospatial data to ongoing rangeland research.
