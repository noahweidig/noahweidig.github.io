I earned my M.S. in Forest Resources & Conservation from the University of Florida's School of Forest, Fisheries, and Geomatic Sciences, graduating with a 4.0 GPA.

My thesis, _Large Wildfire Patterns in the Wildland-Urban Interface_, used geospatial analysis to examine how large wildfires interact with the WUI across the eastern United States. The research earned Outstanding Thesis recognition from both the College of Agricultural & Life Sciences ([Natural Resources](/awards/outstanding-thesis-natural-resources/)) and the School of Forest, Fisheries, & Geomatic Sciences ([Forest Resources & Conservation](/awards/outstanding-thesis-forest-resources/)).

The thesis is available as [Patterns and drivers of large wildfires](/publications/weidig-patterns-drivers-25/), and its main findings were published in the [International Journal of Wildland Fire](/publications/weidig-changing-large-24-gmcj/). I did the work as a [graduate research assistant](/experience/uf-grad-research/) funded by a [Graduate Research Assistantship](/awards/graduate-research-assistantship/), and I built the [WUI Wildfire Explorer](/projects/wuirisk/) from the data. Related awards: [Lowe Scholarship](/awards/lowe-scholarship/), [Outstanding Teaching Assistant](/awards/outstanding-teaching-assistant/), [IFAS Travel Grant](/awards/ifas-travel-grant/), and [Best Student Presentation](/awards/best-student-presentation/).
