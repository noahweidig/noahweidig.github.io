I earned my M.S. in Forest Resources & Conservation from the University of Florida's School of Forest, Fisheries, and Geomatic Sciences, graduating with a 4.0 GPA.

My thesis, _Large Wildfire Patterns in the Wildland-Urban Interface_, used geospatial analysis to examine how large wildfires interact with the WUI across the eastern United States. The research earned Outstanding Thesis recognition from both the College of Agricultural & Life Sciences and the School of Forest, Fisheries, & Geomatic Sciences.
