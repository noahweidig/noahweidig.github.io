I received the Best Student Presentation Award at the International Association of Landscape Ecology North America in 2025. There, I gave a talk, [Fire at the fringe](/publications/weidig-fire-fringe-25-f839/), about my master's research, which focused on wildfire risk in the wildland urban-interface (WUI) in the Eastern United States. The research is part of my [M.S. in Forest Resources & Conservation](/education/uf-masters/). My travel to the meeting was covered by the [Student Travel Grant](/awards/student-travel-grant/) and the [BioLEAPS Travel Grant](/awards/bioleaps-travel-grant/).

IALE-NA gives this award for the best student oral or poster presentation, judged at the annual meeting. Only student members of the association are eligible, and past recipients can't win again within the same degree program.
