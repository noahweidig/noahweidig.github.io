My master's thesis on wildfire risk in the wildland-urban interface was recognized as the Outstanding Thesis in Forest Resources & Conservation by the University of Florida's School of Forest, Fisheries, & Geomatic Sciences. This research focused on assessing and mitigating wildfire hazards in the Eastern United States. The thesis is available as [Patterns and drivers of large wildfires](/publications/weidig-patterns-drivers-25/), and its main findings were published in the [International Journal of Wildland Fire](/publications/weidig-changing-large-24-gmcj/). It also won the [Outstanding Thesis in Natural Resources](/awards/outstanding-thesis-natural-resources/) award, and I built the [WUI Wildfire Explorer](/projects/wuirisk/) from the same data.
