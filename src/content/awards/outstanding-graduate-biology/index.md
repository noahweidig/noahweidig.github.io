I was recognized as the Outstanding Graduate in Biology from Northern Kentucky University's Department of Biological Sciences upon completing my undergraduate degree. This honor reflected my academic achievements and contributions to the study of biology, including my [undergraduate research](/experience/nku-research/) on mosquito oviposition, supported by the [Dr. Carol Swarts Milburn Research Fellowship](/awards/milburn-research-fellowship/). It came with my [B.S. in Biological Sciences](/education/nku-bachelors/).
