I was awarded the Dr. Carol Swarts Milburn Research Fellowship from Northern Kentucky University to support my undergraduate research. This fellowship enabled me to pursue an independent research project in the biological sciences: a study of how Culex mosquitoes choose where to lay their eggs, published in PLOS ONE as [The Goldilocks principle](/publications/weidig-goldilocks-principle-22/). I did the work as a [research assistant at NKU](/experience/nku-research/) during my [B.S. in Biological Sciences](/education/nku-bachelors/).
